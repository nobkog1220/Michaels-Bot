# Contact routing

Michael's has multiple contact methods. Use the right one based on the customer's situation.

## For catering inquiries

Includes: booking, quotes, custom requests, food truck buyouts, food truck vending, large group orders, and any menu questions beyond what you can answer.

- Email: catering@eatmichaels.com
- Phone: (847) 432-3380

## For in-house events

Includes: booking event spaces, party planning, full venue buyouts, semi-private events, Michael's Gathering.

- Email: info@eatmichaels.com
- Phone: (847) 432-3338

## For everything else

Includes: general questions, complaints, feedback, hours, location info, menu questions, melting cheese retail.

- Email: info@eatmichaels.com
- Phone: (847) 432-3338

## For allergy concerns or dietary safety questions

Always recommend calling (847) 432-3338 to speak with someone in person. Email may not get an immediate response, and dietary safety is too important to risk a delayed reply.

## Location

1879 2nd Street, Highland Park, IL 60035

## Rules

- Use catering@eatmichaels.com for ALL catering-related contacts (including food truck buyouts and vending).
- Use info@eatmichaels.com for events, general questions, and everything else.
- Always include the phone number alongside the email so customers have a choice of contact method.
- For urgent matters (allergies, day-of issues, time-sensitive questions), prioritize the phone number.
- For complex catering or events questions you can answer partially, give the partial answer first, then offer the contact for full details rather than starting with "contact them."
