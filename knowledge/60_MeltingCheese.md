# Michael's Premium Melting Cheese

## The story

Michael's Grill & Salad Bar is famous for its Melting Cheese — a cold-pack cheddar that lathers on everything from fresh fries to grilled burgers and dogs.

At the end of 2023, the original manufacturer changed their recipe and the cheese stopped melting properly. After an emergency trip to Mars Cheese Castle in Kenosha, Wisconsin to find a temporary solution, Michael's connected with a local cheese manufacturer willing to develop a custom recipe.

After months of tweaking, Michael's Premium Melting Cheese launched as the restaurant's own product. It's a cold-pack cheese spread made with Real Wisconsin Cheese, designed specifically for quality melting.

## Uses

Great on cheese fries, cheese dogs, cheddar burgers, and anything else you want to pour cheese over. It also makes a solid base for béchamel, queso, or beer cheese dip, and works on crackers or pretzel rods.
