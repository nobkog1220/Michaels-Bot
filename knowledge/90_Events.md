# In-house events

Michael's hosts events at its Highland Park location 7 days a week. Three event spaces accommodate groups from 15 to 175 guests (capacity varies by space and seated vs standing configuration — see space descriptions below). Tagline: "Celebrate Highland Park-Style!"

The restaurant has been a North Shore event destination for over 40 years and offers full-service event planning for venue buyouts (no outside planner needed, though they will work with one if the customer already has one).

## Event spaces

Michael's offers three event space options, each with different capacities and pricing.

### Michael's Gathering — $150 rental fee

- Seated capacity: 50
- Reserved seating in the back of the dining area
- Customer works with the team to curate a buffet from the catering menu, along with table reservations

### Semi-Private Event Space — $300 rental fee, $1,500 food & beverage minimum

- Seated capacity: 100 | Standing capacity: 100
- Located in the event space at the back portion of the restaurant
- Ideal for social gatherings, birthday parties, and corporate meetings
- Includes private buffet, full use of the event space, black linen table set-up, 5 flat screen TVs, customizable lighting, and use of the premium sound system

### Full Venue Buyout — $2,900 rental fee

- Seated capacity: 150 | Standing capacity: 175
- Entire restaurant is yours
- Ideal for Bar and Bat Mitzvahs, corporate parties, and social engagements requiring exclusivity
- "Open Counter" menu packages are designed specifically for venue buyouts
- Includes access to nightclub-quality audio visual, customizable lighting, flat screen TVs, dance floor, and DJ booth
- Full-service event planning included (no outside planner needed)

## Food package categories — important context

In-house events have two different sets of food packages depending on event type. Do not mix these up:

- **Counter Menu Packages** — for Full Venue Buyouts only. Guests order food directly from the kitchen crew at the counter.
- **Stationed Menu Packages** — for Semi-Private Events and Michael's Gatherings. Food is set up as a buffet/station.

## Event pricing notes (the fine print)

- Food & beverage minimums apply to all event spaces and depend on date and time
- Staff costs depend on event timing, guest count, and complexity
- Full Venue Buyout: $2,900 venue rental fee includes room set-up, tables, chairs, lighting, and AV
- Semi-Private Space: $300 rental fee plus $1,500 food & beverage minimum
- Security may be required at additional cost based on guest counts
- $1,000 non-refundable deposit required to confirm an event space (applied to final bill)
- 20% service charge added to final bill based on food, beverage, and rentals
- 3.5% credit card processing fee applies to all transactions

## How to discuss in-house events with customers

- Three event spaces exist: Michael's Gathering (50 seated, $150 rental), Semi-Private Event Space (100 seated, $300 rental + $1,500 F&B minimum), Full Venue Buyout (150 seated / 175 standing, $2,900 rental).
- Counter Menu Packages are only for Full Venue Buyouts. Stationed Menu Packages are only for Semi-Private Events and Michael's Gatherings. Do not mix these up.
- Always surface the 20% service charge, $1,000 non-refundable deposit, and 3.5% credit card fee when quoting event pricing — these add up and customers should know upfront.
- Party starters, bar packages, dessert add-ons, and dessert platters can be added to any event package.
- For booking, custom requests, or anything not covered, direct customers to the events team: info@eatmichaels.com or (847) 432-3380.
- This is separate from off-site catering and food truck services — those are different products with different menus and pricing.
