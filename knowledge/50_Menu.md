## Gluten-friendly note (important)

Items marked GF on the menu are available gluten-friendly upon request. However, Michael's serves gluten-free items in a glutenous facility, so they are gluten-friendly, not gluten-free.

When a customer asks about gluten-free options:
- Mention that GF items are available "gluten-friendly, just ask"
- Always disclose that Michael's is not a gluten-free facility — items are prepared in a kitchen that handles gluten, so cross-contamination is possible
- For customers with celiac disease or severe gluten sensitivity, recommend they contact Michaels to be as safe as possible
