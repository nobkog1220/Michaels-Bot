# Food truck services

Michael's has a food truck that can be used for events in two different ways: a private buyout (Michael's team cooks for the host's guests, host pays per person) or vending (Michael's truck operates as a vendor at the host's event, guests purchase individually).

Both options are coordinated through Michael's catering team and require: $1200 food & beverage minimum, space for a 30' truck on level ground, and an outdoor outlet (preferred).

To book either option: catering@eatmichaels.com or (847) 432-3338.

## Food truck buyout

Michael's "Seasoned Grill" team comes to the event and does all the cooking. Pricing covers 2 hours of food service for up to 50 guests. Additional guests require additional staffing fees.

### The Classic Grill — $26 per adult / $12 per child

- Entrées (pick 4): jumbo char dogs, hot dogs, char burgers, chicken teriyaki sandwiches, chicken tenders, turkey burgers, veggie burgers
- Salad (pick 1): Caesar, Greek, Asian, Debbie's, or Garden
- Side: French fries served with Michael's famous Melting Cheese

### The Premium Q — $34 per adult / $12 per child

- Entrées (pick 4): jumbo char dogs, hot dogs, char burgers, chicken teriyaki sandwiches, chicken tenders, turkey burgers, veggie burgers, Italian beef, Polish sausage, Chosen Char dogs
- Salads (pick 2): Caesar, Greek, Asian, Debbie's, or Garden
- Sides (pick 2): French fries with Michael's Melting Cheese, sweet potato fries, seasoned waffle fries, onion rings, mac & cheese, grilled veggies, mashed potato bites, jalapeño poppers, pasta salad

### Buyout add-ons (priced per person)

- Skirt steaks — $10
- Salmon — $10
- Additional side — $5
- Additional salad — $5

### Buyout pricing notes

- Prices exclude tax and 20% service fee
- $1200 food & beverage minimum
- Includes 2 hours of food service for up to 50 guests
- Additional guests require additional staffing fees

## Food truck vending

For events where Michael's Food Truck operates as a vendor — guests order and pay individually at the truck rather than the host paying a per-person catering rate. Common at festivals, public events, or larger gatherings where the host wants a food option without underwriting per-person costs.

Michael's catering team works with the event organizer to put together a menu customized to the event. The list below is a sample menu only — the actual menu varies per event.

### Sample food items

Hot dog, jumbo char dog, char burger, chicken tenders with fries, chicken Caesar salad, fries with cheese, seasoned waffle fries, pizza puff.

### Sample beverages

Beer, wine, Coke, Diet Coke, Sprite, Gatorade can, bottled water.

### Vending pricing notes

- $1200 food & beverage minimum
- Specific item prices are event-specific and set per booking — do not quote prices for vending items
- Menu offerings are customized per event — the items listed above are examples only

## How to discuss food truck services with customers
- This file is the source of truth for food truck buyout and vending. The Food Truck format listed under full-service catering describes a similar setup with slightly different package contents. If a customer asks about a package name that exists in both files (like Premium Q), surface that there are two versions and ask which they're interested in rather than guessing.
- For buyout: the host pays per person and Michael's "Seasoned Grill" team cooks for everyone.
- For vending: guests pay individually and the menu is customized per event. Do not quote specific item prices for vending — those are set per booking.
- Both options require the same logistics: $1200 food & beverage minimum, space for a 30' truck on level ground, and an outdoor outlet (preferred).
- For booking, quotes, or custom menu requests, direct customers to the catering team.
