# Catering

Michael's catering covers graduation parties, office luncheons, and any other occasion. Multiple formats and price points are available — full-service, per-person, à la carte, and gluten-free.

## Catering — Full Service (The Whole Shebang)

Michael's offers full-service catering called "The Whole Shebang."
Tagline: "We grill so you can chill." Michael's brings the food, equipment, and staff to the customer's location.

### Catering formats

There are two formats. Both offer a "Classic Grill" and a "Premium Q" package, with different offerings and pricing.

#### Char Bar (on-site grill setup, staffed by Michael's team)

Classic Grill — $22 per adult / $12 per kid (ages 2-8)
- Mains: hot dogs, jumbo char dogs, char burgers, grilled teriyaki chicken, or chicken tenders
- French fries with Michael's melting cheese
- Garden salad with 2 dressings

Premium Q — $30 per adult / $12 per kid (ages 2-8)
- Mains (pick 4): hot dogs, jumbo char dogs, Polish sausage, char burgers, turkey burgers, veggie burgers, grilled teriyaki chicken sandwiches, chicken tenders, Italian beef, Italian sausage
- Sides (pick 2): french fries with Michael's melting cheese, seasoned waffle fries, sweet potato fries, grilled veggies, onion rings, mac n' cheese, jalapeño poppers
- Salads (pick 2): garden salad, Caesar salad, Asian salad, Greek salad, Debbie's Favorite salad, creamy cole slaw, pasta salad, potato salad

Char Bar pricing notes:
- Prices exclude sales tax, 20% service fee, and $1000 setup fee
- Setup fee includes 2 hours of food service for up to 100 guests

#### Food Truck (Michael's food truck comes to the event)

Classic Grill — $26 per adult / $12 per child
- Mains (pick 4): hot dogs, jumbo char dogs, char burgers, turkey burgers, veggie burgers, grilled teriyaki chicken sandwiches, chicken tenders
- Side: french fries served with Michael's famous melting cheese
- Salad (pick 1): garden salad, Caesar salad, Asian salad, Greek salad, Debbie's Favorite salad

Premium Q — $34 per adult / $12 per child
- Same offerings as the Char Bar Premium Q

Food Truck pricing notes:
- Prices exclude tax and 20% service fee
- Includes 2 hours of food service for up to 50 guests
- Additional guests require additional staffing fees
- $1200 food & beverage minimum
- Space for a 30' truck on level ground and proper ventilation required
- Outdoor outlet preferred

### Beverage & bar packages

Available as add-ons with either catering format:
- Hot chocolate bar
- Beer, wine & cocktails
- Non-alcoholic beverages

Full-service alcohol available.

### Event staffing

Available with or without catering (Michael's can staff an event where the customer provides their own food):
- Grill expert
- Bartender & wait staff
- Clean-up crew

### How to discuss catering with customers

- Share package details, pricing, and what's included when asked.
- Always mention that prices exclude tax and the 20% service fee, plus any applicable setup fee or minimum.
- For booking, quotes, custom requests, or anything beyond standard package info, direct customers to the catering team: catering@eatmichaels.com or (847) 432-3338.

## Per-person catering (Easy Peasy)

Michael's per-person catering, called "Easy Peasy," is designed for feeding a crowd without the stress of guessing portions. Customers pick a package and pay per person.

Tagline: "No sweat. We got you covered."

Gluten-free options are available for both salads and sandwiches.

All family-style packages have a 10-person minimum.

**The Michael's Classic — $22 per person**
- Choice of dog: classic hot dog or jumbo char dog
- Choice of burger: char, turkey, or veggie
- Choice of chicken: grilled or chicken tender
- Cheese fries (upgrade to waffle fries for $1)
- Choice of salad: garden, Greek, Asian, Caesar, or Debbie's Favorite

**The Chicago Classic — $22 per person**
- Hot dogs or jumbo char dogs, Italian beef, Italian sausage
- Cheese fries (upgrade to waffle fries for $1)
- Choice of salad

**The Heavy Apps — $22 per person**
- Choose one app platter: hummus, guac & chips, or bruschetta
- Caprese skewers or pretzel bites
- Chicken skewers or chicken tenders
- Mini dogs or mini burgers
- Mini sandwiches or selection of wraps

**The Walking Taco Bar — $15 per person**
- Fritos / Doritos
- Ground beef / shredded chicken
- Salsa, guacamole, sour cream, onion & cilantro, corn & black bean salsa

**Chili Bar / Baked Potato Bar — $15 per person**
- Toppings: bacon, broccoli, cheddar, chicken, chili, chives, giardiniera, mozzarella, sautéed mushrooms, spinach, sport peppers, teriyaki chicken
- Comes with butter and sour cream on the side

**The Small Bites (Kids Party) — $15 per person**
- Chicken tenders, mini dogs, and mini burgers
- Cheese fries (upgrade to waffle fries for $1)

**The Premium Menu — $30 per person**
- Choose 3 entrées: hot dogs, jumbo char dogs, Polish sausage, char burgers, turkey burgers, veggie burgers, Italian beef, Italian sausage, choice of wrap
- Choose 2 sides: fries with melting cheese, waffle fries, sweet potato fries, onion rings, grilled veggies, mac & cheese

For customers who don't want family-style catering. Michael's can provide an online order form to share with staff so each person places their own order. $15 per lunch.
